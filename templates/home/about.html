About Us

Nexus was founded to simplify the way people buy and sell computer parts and games. We bring together a wide selection of new, used, and hard-to-find components — from motherboards and cooling solutions to graphics cards and retro cartridges — plus a curated catalog of PC and console games.

Our marketplace is built around transparent listings, seller ratings, and secure checkout so buyers can shop confidently and sellers can reach an audience that understands hardware.

At Nexus, we value community, fair pricing, and an obsession with details: clear photos, honest descriptions, and reliable shipping. Whether you're building your first rig or hunting for a collector's gem, Nexus helps you connect, trade, and power up.
